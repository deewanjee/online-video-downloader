Getting started

Set up your workspace and make your first download.

Download guide
Before you beginYour first downloadUnderstand your workspace

Before you begin

  1. Run Start StreamVault.bat on your laptop. Keep its console window open. Open the local address shown by the launcher in your browser.
  2. Check the Engine status in the top bar. FFmpeg/FFprobe and a supported JavaScript runtime must be available for full download and YouTube support.

Your first download

  1. Choose a download folder in Download location. An existing absolute folder path is an alternative if the native picker does not open.
  2. Open the video on its source platform and copy its public video/post URL.
  3. Paste it into New download and choose Find video. Review the title, format and maximum quality.
  4. Choose Add to queue. Follow the Activity panel; a green Downloaded card means the original has completed.
  5. Use Play to watch inside the portal or Folder to open its saved location.

Understand your workspace

  1. Dashboard combines download tools, queue status and recent videos. Video library has search, filters, sorting and selection. Recovery center explains failures and scheduled retries. Help center contains these separate English guides.
  2. A local desktop installation saves into your selected folder. A hosted/server installation prepares media on the server, then Save to device creates a browser download.
