Download multiple videos

Queue up to 50 individual links in one submission.

Prepare the batch

  1. Open Bulk download under New download. Paste one full video URL per line, with no titles or extra words.
  2. Include between 1 and 50 individual links. Exact duplicates within the batch are removed. Use YouTube playlist for a complete playlist.

Choose shared settings

  1. Select output format and maximum quality for the whole batch.
  2. Start with one YouTube transfer for a gentler request rate. Two parallel transfers are available; source limits and your connection still determine speed.
  3. Choose Queue all videos. Invalid or oversized batches add no jobs; fix the message and submit again.

Track results

  1. Each video has its own status. A failure does not discard successful downloads.
  2. Temporary failures can enter Recovery queued and retry after the first-pass queue. Source cooldowns pause requests.
  3. Queue capacity is shared with other work; remove unwanted queued items or wait for running work when capacity is full.
