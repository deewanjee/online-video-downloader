Copy and paste a link

  1. On YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Dailymotion, X or Threads, open the video/post and use Share → Copy link, or copy the address from the browser.
  2. Click the New download URL box. On Windows press Ctrl+V; on macOS press Command+V. Right-click → Paste is also supported.
  3. Paste the video/post URL rather than a search results page, channel page or media file copied from an unrelated site. Threads public post and share URLs are supported when the extractor can resolve them.

Analyze and queue

  1. Choose Find video. Wait for the title and available qualities. Editing the URL cancels the old analysis result.
  2. Pick MP4 for typical video playback or MP3 for audio. Maximum quality is a cap: it cannot improve the source resolution.
  3. Confirm your download folder when prompted, then Add to queue. Green Downloaded means the saved original is ready.

If analysis fails

  1. Read the message and follow Troubleshooting. Verify the same link opens at the source. An unavailable, private, removed or restricted video may require action outside the portal.
  2. For a URL with a YouTube list parameter, use the playlist workflow that opens automatically.
