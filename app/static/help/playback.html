Play downloaded media

Watch originals and prepare a compatible preview when needed.

Open a saved video

  1. Find a green Downloaded card. Hover or focus the thumbnail to reveal Play; touch users can open its details.
  2. Choose Play. Use the native player controls for volume, seeking and fullscreen where supported. Escape or Close stops playback.

Compatible previews

  1. The player first uses original MP4/WebM when supported by the browser. Unsupported playback can request a compatible H.264/AAC preview.
  2. Use Use compatible preview if the original is choppy or unsupported. One preview encodes at a time; download workers remain available.
  3. Preview encoding can use CPU, take time and create an additional derived file. The original remains saved even if preview preparation fails.

Other ways to open media

  1. Use Folder and your desktop media player, or Save to device/Save a copy in server mode.
  2. Audio downloads open in the audio player. A missing original cannot play; reconnect its drive or follow the missing-file message.
