Manage your library

Find completed videos, select across pages and remove unwanted entries.

Download guide
Find a videoSelect and removeUnderstand deletion

Find a video

  1. Use Downloaded to show completed media, Needs attention for failures, or In progress for queued/running work.
  2. In Video library, search by title or platform and choose a sort order. Pagination offers 12, 24, 48 or 100 entries per page.
  3. Green frames and the Downloaded strip indicate completion. Red means action is needed; an amber recovery badge means a temporary failure is scheduled for retry.

Select and remove

  1. Use the checkbox on each removable thumbnail. Select this page and Select all matching apply to the current results.
  2. Selection stays across pages and filters. Check the selected count; Clear resets the selection.
  3. Choose Remove selected. Review the confirmation, including whether original files are kept or deleted, then confirm or Cancel.

Understand deletion

  1. Direct-save originals in your selected folder are kept when removed from the library. Project-owned legacy/server media and cache are deleted. Lifetime download counters stay intact.
  2. Queued work can be removed before it starts. Running downloads and active previews are protected; a partial removal result explains entries that could not be removed.
  3. Again asks for confirmation before creating a fresh job; an existing original is kept.
