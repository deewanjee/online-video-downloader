# Search inside the portal

Find videos and playlists without leaving your workspace.

## Choose a source

1. Open Discover from the sidebar. Enter a title, creator or topic of at least two characters.
2. Choose YouTube videos, YouTube playlists or Dailymotion videos, then Search. Other supported platforms currently use pasted links; universal cross-platform search is not available.
3. Results show source titles, creators and thumbnails. Use Next and Previous for up to five pages of twelve results. Source limits or authentication can prevent results.

## Select and download

1. For videos, select up to fifty results across pages. Choose the shared format and quality, then Download selected. Download queues one result immediately; Choose format opens the detailed analysis workflow.
2. Confirm a destination folder when requested. Existing download settings and queue protections apply. A Downloaded before badge records an earlier successful download; downloading again is allowed.
3. For a playlist choose Review playlist. Review its entry statuses and selection in the playlist panel before queueing.

## Search limits

1. Editing the query, source or result type clears the old selection. Cancel stops waiting for a search response; the current bounded server request may finish in the background.
2. Recent results are cached for five minutes to reduce source requests. Search does not prove that each video is downloadable. Private access, removal, regional limits, DRM and rate limits still apply.
