# Search inside the portal

Find videos and playlists without leaving your workspace.

## Choose a source

1. Open Discover from the sidebar. Enter a title, creator or topic of at least two characters.
2. Choose YouTube videos, YouTube playlists or Dailymotion videos, then Search. Other supported platforms currently use pasted links; universal cross-platform search is not available.
3. Results show source titles, creators and thumbnails. Use Next and Previous for up to five pages of twelve results. Source limits or authentication can prevent results.

## Select and download

1. For videos, select up to fifty results across pages. Choose the shared format and quality, then Download selected. Download queues one result immediately; Choose format opens the detailed analysis workflow.
2. Confirm a destination folder when requested. Existing download settings and queue protections apply. A Downloaded before badge records an earlier successful download; downloading again requires explicit repeat permission.
3. For a playlist choose Review playlist. Review its entry statuses and selection in the playlist panel before queueing.

## Live progress and playback

1. Search thumbnails update from the shared download queue about every two seconds. Queued, Downloading with a percentage, Merging and other finishing stages remain distinct from Downloaded. Green cards indicate a completed saved file; Needs attention includes the source error.
2. Download buttons are disabled while that video is queued/running, so repeated clicks do not create another active copy from Discover. Completed videos move to the Downloaded search tab and Completed library. Enable Allow repeat downloads when you explicitly need another copy. Changing search pages or queries does not stop downloads.
3. Choose Preview or the play icon to watch the source embed without leaving the portal. YouTube and Dailymotion decide whether a video can be embedded; browser autoplay restrictions may require pressing Play inside the frame. Source previews do not download media into your library.
4. After completion, Play saved opens the portal player for the downloaded file. If a previous saved copy exists while another copy downloads, Play saved still uses the completed copy. Closing a source preview unloads the player and stops its playback.
5. If a source blocks embedding, let the download finish and choose Play saved. An earlier download whose library entry was removed shows Downloaded before; it is not claimed to be a playable local file.

## Search limits

1. Editing the query, source or result type clears the old selection. Cancel stops waiting for a search response; the current bounded server request may finish in the background.
2. Recent results are cached for five minutes to reduce source requests. Search does not prove that each video is downloadable. Private access, removal, regional limits, DRM and rate limits still apply.
